My Bhutan Itinerary

Day 1 – Arrival at Phuentsholing

My adventure started at the India–Bhutan border town of Phuentsholing. After completing immigration formalities, I explored the peaceful streets, local markets, and experienced Bhutan's clean and organized environment.

Day 2 – Exploring Thimphu

The scenic drive to Thimphu was filled with rivers, waterfalls, mountains, and lush green forests.

Places I Visited

  • Buddha Dordenma Statue
  • Memorial Chorten
  • Tashichho Dzong
  • Motithang Takin Preserve
  • Traditional handicraft markets
  • Local restaurants and cafés

Thimphu perfectly blends modern life with traditional Bhutanese culture.

Day 3 & Day 4 – Beautiful Punakha

Driving through the famous Dochula Pass was one of the highlights of my trip. The panoramic Himalayan mountain views were absolutely breathtaking.

Places I Explored

  • Punakha Dzong
  • Punakha Suspension Bridge
  • Chimi Lhakhang (Temple of Fertility)
  • Mo Chhu River
  • Traditional Bhutanese villages

Punakha is peaceful, surrounded by green valleys, rivers, and beautiful landscapes.

Day 5 & Day 6 – Amazing Paro

Paro was undoubtedly my favorite destination in Bhutan.

The hike to the world-famous Tiger's Nest Monastery (Paro Taktsang) was challenging but completely worth every step. The monastery, built on the edge of a cliff, offers spectacular views and an unforgettable spiritual experience.

Other Attractions

  • Rinpung Dzong
  • National Museum of Bhutan
  • Kyichu Lhakhang
  • Paro Market
  • Local Bhutanese cafés

Day 7 – Departure

On my final day, I left Bhutan with unforgettable memories, beautiful photographs, and a deep appreciation for the country's culture, nature, and peaceful lifestyle.

"Bhutan is not just a destination; it is an experience that remains with you forever."
